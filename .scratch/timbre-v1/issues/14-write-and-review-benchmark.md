# 14: Write and review the Benchmark

**What to build:** the owner completes the Benchmark. They write 40 Descriptions by hand and review the 60 drafted ones, so the score reflects how real people describe music.

**Blocked by:** 13

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] 40 hand-written Descriptions (pt-BR and English, covering mood, instrument and preference), each with at least 3 expected Songs from the Catalog, added through the template.
- [ ] Each of the 60 drafted Descriptions is reviewed: fixed or replaced as needed, then marked reviewed.
- [ ] The evaluate job is run on the complete Benchmark of 100, and the baseline's score is recorded.
